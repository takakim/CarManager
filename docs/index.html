🛡️ Summary: We do not collect, transmit, monetize, or share your personal data or vehicle logs. All your data resides strictly and exclusively on your personal device.

1. Introduction

Fuel & Energy Tracker ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Handle

The App stores information locally to help you monitor vehicle efficiency, expenses, and mileage:

  • Vehicle Profiles: Make, model, year, fuel/energy type, initial odometer reading, and preferred measurement units.
  • Fuel & Energy Logs: Date/time of fill-up or charging, odometer values, volume/kWh added, price paid, total cost, fuel grade, fuel station name, and optional notes.
  • Historical Analytics: Aggregated consumption patterns, weekly/monthly spending calculations, and fuel efficiency metrics.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email, phone number, physical address, or accounts.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not monitor, track, or access your device's GPS location.
  • No Advertising / Tracking IDs: The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

3. 100% On-Device AI & Intelligence

The App's "Smart Advisor" feature analyzes consumption trends and provides efficiency scores entirely on your device.

  • All algorithmic analysis is calculated 100% locally on your smartphone.
  • Your vehicle logs and history are never transmitted to external AI APIs or cloud servers.

4. Permissions Used

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Reserved for system services and potential user-requested cloud backups. No background telemetry or analytics are transmitted.
  • File Access / Storage Access Framework: Only triggered when you explicitly choose to export or import your records via CSV backup files.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Storage Location: All data is stored in the App's sandboxed local SQLite database on your device.
  • Deletion: You can delete any vehicle or log directly inside the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android Settings completely and permanently removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
