## 1. Introduction

Fuel & Energy Tracker ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We believe that your vehicle and financial data belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

**In summary: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or vehicle telemetry. All your data stays strictly on your personal device.**

---

## 2. Information We Handle

The App allows you to record and track vehicle information locally. All entered data is stored exclusively in a private local database on your Android device:

- **Vehicle Profiles**: Vehicle name, make, model, year, fuel/energy type, initial odometer reading, preferred units (distance, volume, currency).
- **Fuel & Energy Logs**: Date/time of fill-up or charge, odometer readings, volume/energy consumed, unit price, total expense, fuel grade, fuel station name, and optional notes.
- **Calculated Analytics**: Mileage intervals, average fuel economy, spending trends, and cost per distance.

### What We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials.
- **No Location Tracking**: The App does not track, record, or monitor your real-time GPS location.
- **No Device Identifiers or Telemetry**: We do not collect advertising IDs, IMEI numbers, or hardware identifiers.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Ad Networks**: We do not include third-party trackers, ad SDKs, or data brokers.

---

## 3. On-Device AI & Analytics

The App includes a "Smart Advisor" feature providing insights on consumption trends and cost optimizations.

- **100% On-Device Processing**: All calculations, pattern analysis, and recommendations are performed entirely offline using local on-device algorithms.
- **Zero Cloud Transmission**: Your logs and vehicle data are **never** uploaded to external AI servers or third-party cloud APIs.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

The App requests minimal Android permissions:

- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**: Reserved for system services and potential user-requested cloud backups. No background telemetry or analytics are transmitted.
- **Storage / File Access (Storage Access Framework)**: Used strictly when you explicitly choose to export or import your data via CSV backup files. The App only accesses files you explicitly pick through Android's system file picker.

---

## 5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

- **Local Storage**: All your data is kept in the App's sandboxed SQLite database on your device.
- **Complete Control**: You have full control over your data:
  - You can edit or delete individual logs at any time.
  - You can delete entire vehicle profiles and their histories within the App.
  - Clearing App Storage or uninstalling the App immediately and permanently erases all data stored by the App.
- **Backups**: You can generate complete CSV backups at any time. You choose where to store these backups (e.g., local storage, Google Drive, email).
