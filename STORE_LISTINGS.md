```

```

### Full description
```text
Fuel & Energy Tracker is your all-in-one vehicle cost companion. Whether you drive a petrol, diesel, hybrid, LPG, or 100% electric vehicle (EV), easily log fill-ups and charging sessions, track real-time fuel economy, and uncover your vehicle's true total cost of ownership.

🚗 Key Features:

Multi-Fuel & EV Ready:
Track gasoline, diesel, ethanol, LPG, CNG, or electric charging (kWh). Switch seamlessly between metric (L, km, L/100km, km/L) and imperial (gal, mi, MPG, mi/kWh) units with customizable currencies.

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):
Track vehicle purchase price, cumulative running expenses, and cost-per-kilometer/mile to understand the real financial picture of each vehicle in your garage.

Expense & Budget Breakdown:
View spending summaries organized by custom timeframes: weekly, monthly, yearly, and all-time. Instantly see where your vehicle budget goes.

Unit Price & Inflation Trends:
Visualize fuel and electricity price fluctuations over time with interactive historical charts to spot seasonal patterns and save on fill-ups.

Multi-Vehicle Garage & Archive:
Manage multiple personal and family vehicles under one app. Retain complete historical records even after selling or trading in a vehicle.

Smart Driving & Efficiency Insights:
Get automated smart summaries on driving habits, consumption deviations, and cost-saving tips.

Data Privacy & Easy Backup:
Enjoy full offline access with secure local storage. Easily export and import your logs in standard CSV format anytime.

💡 Perfect For:
• Everyday drivers looking to optimize commuting and road-trip expenses.
• EV owners wanting an accurate breakdown of home and public charging costs.
• Car enthusiasts and fleet owners keeping complete service and fuel histories.
```
